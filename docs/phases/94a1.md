# Reordering from mull serve

## Design

Jeff moves an item up or down within its group from its sheet. The page sends a `rank` op (the whole new order, as ed34 requires) through the ops endpoint, saved as Jeff; `rank` joins the ops mull serve takes. It follows DESIGN.md's write buttons and keyboard rules.

## Done when

Jeff can reorder a phase's items in mull serve, and the CLI's `mull ready` shows the new order.
