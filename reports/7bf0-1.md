## Summary

Needs now exist between phases only. A phase may need other phases; items have no needs and are unordered inside a phase, so an item is ready when it is committed and its phase's needs are done (or it has no phase). need/unneed on an item, add --needs, and a phase needing an item fail with not_allowed, and need/unneed always print {type: phase, phase}. Item needs already on the mull branch are kept and shown but ignored for readiness and cycles; a phase's existing needs on items still count and unneed clears them; mull migrate (488d) converts both. json.md, the intake and supervisor guides, design.md and the README are updated to match.
