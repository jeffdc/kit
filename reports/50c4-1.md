## What was built

### Summary

Finished unplanned work is archived automatically (Jeff's decision): an unplanned item that is done or dropped, with nothing open or parked on it, leaves Unplanned at once, is listed in the sheet index's Archive under a new Unplanned group (newest first, opening its sheet, opening by itself on that sheet), is left out of default Find with the entries on it, and its sheet reads 'in the Archive'. Unplanned's Show all still lists it. Items in open plans still age out of the tree after 14 days. Docs (DESIGN.md Archive and Find, design.md, guide json, the model sheet) and the dev demo updated.

## How it was built

### Design notes

Approach: archiving unplanned work is derived, never stored, as Jeff asked (no new state or flag). One predicate in internal/mull/pages.go, archivedUnplanned(i) = unplanned(i) && finished(i) && !held(i), where unplanned is "no plan and not captured", finished is done or dropped (graph.go), and held is "an entry on it is open or parked" (the rule agedOut already had, pulled out as held). Every place that needs the answer calls it, so Unplanned, the tree's Archive, search and the item sheet can never disagree: an item is either live or archived, never both.

- agedOut now returns true at once for finished unplanned work (no 14-day wait) and keeps the 14-day rule only for items in plans, so counts() and the tree's listable filter are unchanged in shape.
- archived(i) = in a closed plan, or archivedUnplanned. It drives ItemPage.archived, so an item in a closed plan's sheet also says "in the Archive".
- UnplannedPage: aged_out and done_shown_days are replaced by archived (the count left out); ?done=all still lists them.
- TreePage: archived_unplanned [{id, title, state, finished}], newest first by updated, beside archive (closed plans). Kept as a separate field rather than reshaping archive, so the existing closed-plan shape and its tests stay as they were.
- Search (search.go searchDocs): an item hit is Archived when its plan is closed or archivedUnplanned(item); an entry hit is Archived when its plan (its own, or else its item's) is closed, or its item is archivedUnplanned. A phase or plan hit only through a closed plan. Search() leaves Archived hits out unless archive=true and counts them in Found.Archived.
- cache.go index(): byItem is now built before the search docs, since archivedUnplanned reads an item's entries; otherwise each item would scan every entry.

UI: tree.js's Archive row shows when it has closed plans or archived unplanned items, its meta counting both; an Unplanned group (key au:<pid>, collapsed) lists the items with their ID (copy button as every tree item row) and "done/dropped <date>", each opening the item's sheet; expandFor opens Archive and the group on such an item's sheet. item.js adds "in the Archive" after the stage tag. lists.js's note points to the Archive and keeps "Show them here too".

Rejected: a stored archived flag or an archive op (Jeff ruled it out); archiving finished items with open entries anyway and listing them in both places (confusing, and would hide open questions from default search); reshaping Tree.archive into a union of plans and items (breaks its shape for no gain).

Tests: archive_test.go (done, dropped, open-question and parked holds, settling a question archives, search counts with and without the archive, dropped capture behaviour for 522d, closed-plan items); aging_test.go now covers only planned work; serve api_test.go checks the wire shape of archived, archived_unplanned and the item's archived. Tried with make dev (demo seeds now include unplanned work): screenshots of the archived item sheet, the tree's Archive, Unplanned with and without Show all, and Find leaving it out with "Search the archive too: 2 more".

### Decisions made in the session

Accepting the review ratifies these. To decide one differently, before accepting: `mull decide <id> "..."`

- **c07e** Finished unplanned work is archived automatically: an unplanned item (committed, no plan) that is done (accepted) or dropped is in the Archive right away, with no new state or flag in the model
  Why: Jeff's decision, 2026-11-15: for unplanned work, done or dropped = archived. It leaves Unplanned at once (no 14-day fade), is listed under an Unplanned group in the sheet index's Archive, left out of default search, and its sheet says it's in the Archive. Items in open plans are unchanged.
- **8419** An unplanned item with an entry on it still open or parked isn't archived yet: it stays in Unplanned, in default search and out of the Archive until the entry is settled, then moves to the Archive (the rule agedOut already had)
  Why: One predicate (archivedUnplanned in pages.go) drives Unplanned, the tree's Archive, search and the item sheet, so an item is never both live and archived, and open questions on it stay visible
- **28f6** Unplanned's Show all stays: #/<project>/unplanned/all (?done=all) still lists the archived work in the table too; the note under the table now says how many done or dropped items are in the Archive, at the foot of the sheet index, with Show them here too
  Why: The simpler consistent thing: the route and its API already exist, and the tree's Archive is a list, not a sheet a link could open
- **1073** The Unplanned page's API drops aged_out and done_shown_days for archived (the count left out); the tree gains archived_unplanned [{id, title, state, finished}], newest first; an item page gains archived (true in a closed plan too); all documented in mull guide json
  Why: Nothing on Unplanned ages any more, so the 14-day fields meant nothing
- **4229** An item in a closed plan also reads 'in the Archive' after its stage tag on its sheet, as finished unplanned work does
  Why: Search already treated it as archived; the sheet now says the same for both
- **9564** Search treats the entries on archived unplanned work (its review, its decisions, its settled questions) as archived too, as it does a closed plan's
  Why: Consistent with records in closed plans; an open or parked entry keeps its item out of the Archive, so nothing open is hidden
- **12b9** The dev demo (scripts/dev-demo.sh) gains unplanned work: one committed, one done and one dropped, so the Archive's Unplanned group shows with make dev
  Why: The demo had no unplanned work and no closed plan, so the Archive never showed there
- **0aa9** A captured item dropped with no plan (bug 522d) is archived like finished unplanned work: it leaves the Unplanned list and count at once and is listed in the Archive's Unplanned group; its sheet's crumb still says Unplanned
  Why: mull can't tell a dropped capture from dropped committed work (nothing records that it was committed), so 522d stays open for Jeff; this makes it less visible, not worse
