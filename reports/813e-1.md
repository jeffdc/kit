## How it was built

### Design notes

The similarity lives in a new file, internal/mull/similar.go, so it stays out of the way of the other small-fix items touching ops.go (fe34 changes CaptureItemIn's fields; this item changes only the line that returns its result). The bug and idea batch op returns CaptureView, an ItemView with a Similar list embedded so the JSON stays the flat ItemView plus one key. CaptureItem (used only by tests) still returns ItemView.

Scoring: each title becomes a set of words (lower case, split on anything but letters and digits, a small stopword list and single letters dropped, a plural -s or -es trimmed). Candidates are the captured and committed items other than the new one. Each word is weighted ln(1 + N/df), N the candidates plus the new title and df how many of them hold the word, and two titles score a weighted Dice overlap (2 x shared weight / both weights). They're similar when they share two words (one if either title has only one) and score at least 0.5, or share four or more words and score at least 0.35.

Why that shape: plain unweighted overlap flagged every pair of short titles sharing a project prefix ("Backlog view: filtering" against "Backlog view: sort by column", "mull serve: ..."). Rarity weighting fixes that. A single threshold then missed long near-duplicates, which share many words but also carry many of their own, so long titles sharing four or more words get a lower bar. I tried the rule (re-implemented in a scratch script) on kit's 73 open captured and committed titles: it finds 5 pairs, 4 of them real near-duplicates or the same issue (e.g. 0f73/e244 on committer UTC offsets, 16d6/cd46 on naming changed doc sections, 4a05/e296 on design.md's epic leftovers) and the fifth closely related (8817/8e3e, integration review), with none of the prefix noise.

Rejected: embedding or fuzzy-string libraries (a dependency for a hint), Jaccard (dilutes long titles even more than Dice), comparing notes as well as titles (the item asks for titles, and notes are long and noisy), and listing active or in-review items (a capture about work in progress is usually a new finding in it). Tests: internal/mull/similar_test.go covers word splitting, reordering and plurals, committed work counting, stages left out, the prefix discount, long titles, the cap of five and [] when none; cmd/shapes_test.go checks the printed keys.
