## What was built

### Summary

Find: press s anywhere outside a text field (or click Find in the brand row) and a band opens at the top of the sheet that searches every registered project by ID and title. Exact IDs come first, then IDs it starts, then titles whose words start with the query's, then titles containing them. Up/Down move, Enter or a click opens the record's sheet (an inbox entry opens its item's or plan's), Esc closes. The band sits in place rather than floating (DESIGN.md: nothing floats but the toast), and DESIGN.md now describes it. Server: mull.Search over an index built once per cached state, and GET /api/search across projects. ~32ms over HTTP on the large seed for a word in nearly every title.

### UI tour

1. make dev, open http://localhost:6899/#/demo.
2. Press s: the Find band opens at the top of the sheet, cursor in the box.
3. Type part of a title (e.g. 'search'): matching plans, phases, items and inbox entries list with their project, kind and stage; the first is selected.
4. Press Down to move the selection, Enter to open it: its sheet opens and the band closes.
5. Press s again, type an item's ID (e.g. one from the sheet index): it's the first hit. Press Esc: the band closes.
6. Click Find in the brand row: the same band opens. At phone width (narrow the window) the Find button sits beside Sheet index.
7. make dev-stop.

## How it was built

### Design notes

Server. mull.Search(q, limit) scans searchDocs, every plan, phase, item and inbox entry as a Hit plus its ID and title lower-cased, built once when a state enters the cache (state.search) and on the fly for a state being written. match ranks a record: 0 for the exact ID, 1 for an ID the query starts (single-word queries only), 2 when every word starts a word of the title, 3 when every word appears in it, else no match. Ties go to the most recently changed (an entry's settlement counts as a change). Entries carry their item and plan, so the UI knows where to open them. GET /api/search fans out to every registered repository concurrently, prefixes each hit with its project ID, stable-sorts by rank (so project order holds among equals) and returns the best 30. Unreadable repositories are left out rather than failing the search.

I rejected an inverted token index: about 16k records on the large seed scan in ~20ms, and a scan needs no upkeep across the incremental reloads.

UI. search.js owns a band (#search) at the top of <main>; the sheet now renders into #main (display: contents, so layout is unchanged), so live re-renders never wipe the box or the selection. The band is in place rather than an overlay because DESIGN.md allows nothing to float but the toast; it pushes the sheet down, on Panel Grey under a 1.5px ink rule. Input is debounced 60ms and responses are sequence-numbered so a slow early answer can't overwrite a later one. s opens it unless focus is in an input, textarea, select or contenteditable, or a modifier is held; a Find button in the brand row opens it too, since a key alone can't be discovered (and phones have none). Rows are links, so cmd-click opens a new tab; a plain click or Enter navigates and closes. Settled entries show their settlement in past tense (accepted, decided…).

Checked in headless Chromium against make dev serving the large seed: band open with results and selection, Enter navigating to an item and revealing it in the sheet index, and phone width (where a plan slug in the ID column first wrapped; the column is now 84px with an ellipsis). make bench has a search row.
