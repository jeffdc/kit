# Done report 1: Review buttons in Needs you are a no-op on the item's own sheet, and elsewhere open the item at its top, not its review packet (8ff5)

**Status:** PASSED  
**Commit:** eb69ff6d1356

## What was built

### Summary

Review buttons and review rows in Needs you open the item scrolled to its review packet, as stamps open a sheet at its Needs you; on the item's own sheet, where they did nothing, they scroll down to it.

### UI tour

1. On a project's overview, click Review on a row in Waiting for your review: the item opens scrolled to its review packet.
2. On that item's own sheet, scroll to the top and click Review in its Needs you (the review row, or a decision group's Review): the page scrolls down to the packet.
3. Clicking anything else that changes sheet still opens it at the top.

### Checks (run by mull)

- `make done-checks`: pass, 3.4s

## How it was built

### Design notes

How: app.js gains focusReview beside focusNeeds. A Review link (data-review on the review row's title link and button, the row itself, and a review-carried group's Review) sets it to the item's hash; if that's the current sheet, showReview scrolls to section.review at once, otherwise the hash change renders the item and render() scrolls when the packet is drawn (and keeps trying until the page has loaded it). The hash-change handler now decides before rendering whether the sheet is opened somewhere (a stamp or a Review link) and only otherwise scrolls to the top; before, it scrolled to the top after render, which would have undone a scroll the render made. Checked in headless Chromium at 500px tall: all four Review paths land on the packet. The commit (eb69ff6) was made on db04's branch just after db04 was accepted, so its trailer says db04.
