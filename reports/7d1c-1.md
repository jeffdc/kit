# Done report 1: Clicking Approve on an individual decision in mull serve doesn't seem to work: the button briefly turns black, then white again (7d1c)

**Status:** PASSED  
**Commit:** 7a7f1aab561b  
**By:** /Users/jeff/dev/kit at 2026-11-15T19:34:18Z

## What was built

### Summary

Approve on one decision now saves on the first click, with no armed 'Approve?' step. Jeff's report: one click turned the button black and then white again, and nothing was saved; that was the confirm step arming and timing out after 4s, which read as broken. Approve all N still asks first.

### UI tour

1. make install, open kit's Overview and open Decisions to approve (or open f7ca's sheet and scroll to Decisions made in this session).
2. Click Approve once on one decision: it reads Saving… for a moment, the toast says 'Approved decision <id>.', and the row leaves.
3. Hover an Approve: its tooltip says 'Saves to mull in one click.'
4. On a group with several open decisions not carried by a review, click 'Approve all': it still turns ink and asks 'Approve N?'; a second click saves.

### Checks (run by mull)

- `make done-checks`: pass, 20.9s

## How it was built

### Design notes

Cause: not a fault in saving. Every write button arms on its first click (inked, "Approve?") and saves on the second; Esc, a click elsewhere or 4 seconds disarms it. Jeff clicked once, saw the ink and saw it fade back, and nothing was written. I reproduced every Approve on the dev demo (Needs you, a group of one, the review packet, after a send-back) and each saved on two clicks, and measured the buttons on Jeff's real kit pages while armed (only arming, nothing written): they shift at most 7px, so a second click would have landed.

Change: writeButton takes confirm (default true). With confirm false it marks the button data-now and titles it "Saves to mull in one click."; write() in app.js skips arming for such a button and saves at once, through the same save() path (pending "Saving…", conflict redraw, failure kept in place, toast). needs.js's approve() passes confirm: ids.length > 1, so a decision's own Approve and a group of one's header save on one click while Approve all N still arms; item.js's review packet passes confirm: false. DESIGN.md's Two Kinds of Action Rule names the exception and why.

Rejected: keeping the confirm and dropping the 4s timeout (Jeff chose one click); making every write one-click (Accept, Close and archive and Approve all settle more than one thing, and Jeff's report was about single decisions).

Checked in headless Chromium against make dev: one click on each kind of single Approve saved and toasted; Approve all on a group of two armed and asked "Approve 2?".
