## What was built

### Summary

Questions are answered and decisions decided differently from mull serve: a write that takes text opens a form in place (the second write kind's text variant, in DESIGN.md), saving through decide as the human. Question rows get Answer beside Discuss with an agent (the old copy); decisions get Decide differently in Needs you and in the review packet.

### UI tour

1. Open a project with an open question in Needs you: the row has Discuss with an agent (copies a prompt, as Answer used to) and Answer.
2. Click Answer: a form opens under the question with the cursor in it; type an answer and press Cmd-Enter (or Save answer). A toast says Answered <id>, the question leaves Needs you, and mull decisions shows your answer by your git user.name with human true.
3. Open Answer again on another question, type something, press Esc: the form closes; click Answer again and your draft is still there.
4. In Decisions to approve, hover a decision: Decide differently appears beside Approve; write your decision and save, and it leaves the group, in the decision log in place of the agent's.
5. Open an item in review: each open decision in the packet has Decide differently; deciding one shows it as DECIDED with your decision under it, and Accept's count drops by one.

## How it was built

### Design notes

How: write.js gains textWrite(), which returns the button and the form separately, so each caller places the form where it reads best (under a question's text, spanning a decision row, under a packet decision). Its state shares the write map: {open, draft, pending?, error?}. disarm() now also closes open forms that aren't saving, keeping their drafts, so Esc and a click elsewhere treat armed buttons and open forms alike.

app.js: write()'s save path became save(key, pid, ops, done, keep), shared by buttons and forms; keep is what survives pending and failure (the form's open state and draft), and a conflict keeps an open form's draft too. Handlers: data-form-open toggles a form (closing any other), data-form-close closes, submit fills the text into each op's field and saves, input mirrors the draft into state and enables Save, Cmd/Ctrl-Enter submits. render() keeps the caret: it records the focused textarea's selection and restores it after focusKey (which now knows data-draft and data-form-open) refocuses the replacement.

Placement keys: an item's sheet shows its decisions in its Needs you and in the review packet, and one key opened both forms and focused the hidden one; decideDifferently takes a where ("needs", "packet") into its key. Found in the headless run.

Server: uiOps offers decide; a test answers a question and decides a decision differently in one request and checks the log (by the human, human true, the agent's text kept as context).

Docs: DESIGN.md's Two Kinds of Action Rule covers text writes, the text write component, question rows, the decision tier and the rule that writes are never primary; json.md, READMEs and the model sheet name the new writes.

Checked in headless Chromium on a scratch repo: answering with a live re-render landing mid-typing (draft and focus kept, Cmd-Enter saved, the log shows it by jeffdc, human true); Decide differently in the review packet (DECIDED with the text, Accept's count down by one).

### Decisions made in the session

Accepting the review ratifies these. To decide one differently, before accepting: `mull decide <id> "..."`

- **2871** Esc, Cancel or a click elsewhere closes a text form but keeps its draft for next time; drafts and the caret survive live re-renders
  Why: Losing typed text to a stray key or an agent's write landing mid-word would be the worst failure of a form.
- **317f** Decide differently is offered on every decision, carried by a review or not, in Needs you as in the review packet
  Why: Deciding one differently before accepting a review is the supervisor guide's own flow.
- **b4b4** Question rows keep the old copy action as Discuss with an agent, beside the new Answer write
  Why: Handing a question to an agent stays a copy; answering it yourself is the write.
- **d1dc** Writes are never primary (.p): an inked write means armed or open, so the Blocking tier's emphasis stays on its highlighted title
  Why: Primary and armed are both ink fills; giving writes .p would make an idle write look armed.
- **d4a6** A write that takes text doesn't arm: its button opens a form in place, and that opening is the asking once more; Save writes at once
  Why: Typing and saving is already two deliberate steps; arming the Save too would be a third click for nothing.
