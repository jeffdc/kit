## What was built

### Summary

Closes the hole Jeff hit: deciding one of a review's decisions differently, then approving the rest, accepted the work. Now mull's decide sends the review back when a decision on in-review work is decided differently, with the decision and instructions in the item's notes, so the work can't be accepted (page or CLI) until an agent takes the change in, says how it'll make it (mull decided), and files it for review again. Each open decision gets its own Approve, in the review packet and in Needs you's review-carried groups, settling that decision only. 'Accept with its N decisions' is unchanged otherwise. In the packet, Decide differently's save reads 'Save and send the work back'.

### UI tour

1. make dev, open http://localhost:6899/#/demo and open the Digest job item (in review, two decisions).
2. In the review packet, each decision has Approve and Decide differently; Accept with its 2 decisions is unchanged.
3. Click Decide differently on the first decision: the save button reads 'Save and send the work back'. Type a decision and save: the toast says the work goes back; the packet header shows SENT BACK with your decision, the stage is Ready, and the Notes carry it.
4. Click Approve on the other decision (then again to save): it shows APPROVED; the item stays Ready, not done.
5. make dev-reset is not needed: delete .dev/demo and run make dev for a fresh demo. make dev-stop.

## How it was built

### Design notes

The rule lives in mull, not the page: decide() settles the decision, then, if it's a decision on an item in review with an open (or parked) review, calls sendBack on that review with a reason naming the decision, its text and the new decision, and saying what the agent should do. sendBack is the review branch of Do, extracted so both use it: the review settles as do, the item returns to committed with the claim cleared and the reason appended to its notes, and its decisions stay open. Because the item isn't in review any more, Accept on it fails with the existing state error, from the page or the CLI, until an agent starts it, takes the decision in, and finishes again (which files a new review carrying whatever's still open). Decisions on work that isn't in review are decided as before.

I chose 'any open review on the decision's item' over 'only decisions the review carries': a decision filed during review is still about that work, and deciding it differently means the same thing.

UI: the packet gives every open decision an Approve (writeButton, accept op on that decision alone) beside Decide differently, in a .decn-acts wrapper so both sit in the action cell, with its failure line in the text column. decideDifferently takes inReview: in the packet while the review is open, and in Needs you's review-carried groups, its save reads 'Save and send the work back', and its placeholder and toast say the work goes back. Needs you's carried groups now give each decision its own Approve too (they had none, by the old 'settled by reviewing it' rule). The armed Accept's note now says to decide a wrong decision differently instead.

Tests: TestDecidingDifferentlySendsReviewBack (sent back with the decision in the notes; accepting the work fails; the other decision approves alone and leaves the item committed); TestDecidingOutsideReviewSendsNothingBack. TestReviewCarriesDecisions and TestItemPageReviewPacket encoded the old 'decide then accept' flow and now check the send-back instead. Checked in headless Chromium on the demo: Approve and Decide differently on each decision, the save label, the toast, SENT BACK with the reason, Ready, and approving the other decision alone.

DESIGN.md and docs/design.md describe the rule.

### Decisions made in the session

Accepting the review ratifies these. To decide one differently, before accepting: `mull decide <id> "..."`

- **1c22** 'Accept with its N decisions' is unchanged when nothing was decided differently; once one is, the review is already sent back, so Accept isn't offered
  Why: Jeff asked to leave Accept as it is; the send-back is what blocks it.
- **2328** mull itself enforces it: deciding a decision differently while its item is in review sends the review back (the item returns to committed, the decision in its notes), so neither the page nor an agent on the CLI can accept the work until it's done again
  Why: Jeff's rule: acceptance isn't possible until the agent takes the change in and decides how to make it, then it's reopened for review. Putting it in mull's decide rather than only the page closes the hole for agents too.
- **5d08** Each open decision gets its own Approve, in the review packet and in Needs you's groups a review carries; it settles that decision only, never the work
  Why: Jeff: all of the other decisions can be accepted, so having an approve button for each would make it work.
