## What was built

### Summary

mull decided "..." --human --on-request records a decision Jeff made in conversation: a decision entry settled at once as decided (note = the text, by the worktree, on_request), so it goes straight into the decision log and context, never into the inbox, and no review carries it. Refused without --on-request (not_allowed); --on-request without --human is invalid_argument. Batch decided takes human and on-request; ask doesn't. design.md, the agents and supervisor guides, mull guide json and kit's AGENTS.md describe it.

## How it was built

### Design notes

The decided op got its own input, DecidedIn, with the capture fields plus human and on-request; ask keeps CaptureIn. Batch decodes with DisallowUnknownFields, so ask still refuses those fields rather than silently taking them, and the CLI adds --human and --on-request only to decided.

The logic is one function, Mull.decided in internal/mull/inbox.go. Without --human it is the old capture (and refuses a stray --on-request with invalid_argument). With --human it checks m.asked(onRequest) (so mull serve's AsHuman would also pass, settling by Jeff's name with human: true, though serve doesn't offer decided), captures the entry with all the usual rules (text, scope, item to plan), then settles it through the existing settle() with the decide action and the text as the note. Reusing settle keeps By, OnRequest, Human and At exactly as for decide --on-request, so the decision log, context's item_decisions and the change feed need no changes: the entry is in Decisions at once, openDecisions skips it (so done's review doesn't carry it), and the inbox never lists it.

Rejected: a new settlement action or an entry field marking "Jeff's own". Decide already means "Jeff wrote the decision"; on_request plus by records that an agent recorded it. Also rejected: routing through decide(), whose send-back on items in review is for overruling a decision, not adding one.

Tests: internal/mull/decided_test.go (refusals, settlement shape, decision log, not in inbox, not carried by a review, AsHuman, batch incl. all-or-nothing and ask refusing the field) and cmd TestUsageErrorsHaveACode (flags, codes, output).
