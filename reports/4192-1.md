# Done report 1: Find as a floating command palette in the middle of the screen, not a band at the top (the human's decision on d035) (4192)

## What was built

### Summary

Find is now a floating command palette in the middle of the screen, as decided on d035, instead of a band that pushed the sheet down. It floats flat over a scrim (a 1.5px ink box, no shadow, at most 680px wide, about a seventh of the way down); a click on the scrim or Esc closes it and gives focus back. Search, keys and results are unchanged. DESIGN.md now allows the toast and Find to float and describes the palette.

### UI tour

1. make dev, open http://localhost:6899/#/demo.
2. Press s: the palette floats mid-screen over a dimmed sheet, cursor in the box; the sheet doesn't move.
3. Type part of a title: results list in the palette; Down moves the selection, Enter opens it and the palette closes.
4. Press s again and click the dimmed area outside the palette: it closes, and the page is where it was.
5. Narrow the window to phone width and click Find: the palette fills the width with a 16px margin.
6. make dev-stop.

## How it was built

### Design notes

Only the presentation changed; search.js's search, ranking, keys and navigation are as in b9f0.

The palette is a fixed layer (#search, beside the toast at the body level) holding a scrim and the panel. The scrim is ink at 28% (color-mix, so it follows dark mode); the panel is a 1.5px ink box on the ground colour with no shadow or radius, so it floats without breaking DESIGN.md's flat, rule-built depth. It sits about 14vh down rather than centred vertically, so a growing result list doesn't make the input jump, and it caps at 72vh with the list scrolling inside. A click on the scrim or Esc closes it; closing returns focus to whatever had it, except after opening a record, where the new sheet takes focus.

Because the palette no longer lives inside <main>, the sheet goes back to rendering into <main id="main"> directly and the display: contents wrapper is gone.

DESIGN.md: the Elevation rule now names two floating things (the toast and Find) and why; the Find section describes the palette; the More toggle's wording no longer leans on 'nothing floats'.

Checked in headless Chromium against make dev with the large seed: the palette at 1400px and 420px, the selection fill, Enter navigating to an item, and a scrim click closing it.
