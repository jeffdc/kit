## What was built

### Summary

Needs you entries can be parked or dropped from mull serve: questions and decisions get More, which shows Park and Drop in place, each taking a reason and saving as the human. Question rows now open their forms full width under the row. The server offers drop and park.

### UI tour

1. Open a project with an open question in Needs you and click More on its row: Park and Drop appear, and More reads Less.
2. Click Park, write why it can wait, and Park it: a toast says it's parked, and it leaves Needs you (counted as parked).
3. In Decisions to approve, hover a decision and click More, then Drop: the form's button reads Drop it for good; save it and the decision is gone.
4. Open Answer on a question: the form spans the row under the question instead of squeezing beside the buttons.

- `make done-checks`: pass, 3.3s

## How it was built

### Design notes

How: write.js gains a More toggle: a Set of open keys, moreButton() and toggleMore(), kept across live re-renders like the write states. needs.js's parkOrDrop() returns the buttons and forms for an entry: two textWrites (park with the reason; drop with ids and the reason) shown when More is open, or while either form is open, so a click elsewhere that closes the form doesn't hide what you were using. Question rows and decision rows both use it; review rows don't (reviews are settled by Accept and Send it back).

Layout: the question row's form moved out of the text cell to a grid row of its own (columns 3 to the end), and .it .act wraps, right-aligned, within 380px; at 860px and below the actions sit under the text, left-aligned. The open More takes the Panel Grey fill and stays visible in decision groups, whose row actions otherwise show on hover.

Server: uiOps offers drop and park; a test parks a question and drops a decision in one request and checks both are settled by the human with the reason.

Docs: DESIGN.md (rows and the decision tier), json.md, the model sheet.

Checked in headless Chromium on a scratch repo: park a question (toast, gone from Needs you) and drop a decision (toast); the first run showed the cramped question row, fixed and rechecked.
