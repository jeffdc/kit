### Summary

### Design notes

Server: TreePage now places each open entry once, where the tree shows it. It first lists the rows (every committed item in a phase, and a plan's committed items with no phase as unfiled), then walks the open entries: on a listed item it counts on the item; on unplanned work on Unplanned; otherwise on its plan (the entry's, or its item's) as the plan's own, and entries for plans the tree doesn't show (done) or no plan become the project's own. Phase, plan and tree counts are sums of their rows, so they add up by construction, and Blocking is summed the same way. TreeItem gains needs_you and blocking; TreePlan gains unfiled and own; Tree gains unplanned and own (Attention: {needs_you, blocking}). The test asserts the sums at every level and that the total equals the overview's open count. mull guide json documents the placement rule.

UI: marks(blocking, total, long, link) now wraps both stamps in one link (a.mk, data-needs). The tree adds stamps to item rows, a 'No phase' group under a plan (its items, linking to the plan), and 'About the plan' / 'About the project' rows only when they hold something. A done item that holds something isn't faded. Clicking a stamp records the target hash in state.focusNeeds; once that sheet renders its Needs you (id needs on the overview, plan Status, phase, item and Unplanned sheets), showNeeds opens every tier there and scrolls to it; if you're already on that sheet it does so at once, and any other navigation clears it. On the item sheet Needs you now comes before the review packet, so the jump lands on it. Violet is --vi/--vi-ink in the light, dark and data-theme blocks, filled like the yellow stamp.
