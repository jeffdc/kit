Later calls and later writes are cheap (the walk is kept and extended), but the first page load after being away pays it. Tried and rejected: a walk limited to the changed records' paths (git log -- <7582 paths>) is far slower, since git matches every pathspec per entry; it ran over 10 minutes. Remaining option, the human's call: time a change from the record's own fields (updated, created, settled.at) instead of commit times; docs have no such field.
