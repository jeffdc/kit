Jeff asked to drop phase 7195 (mull-ui-edit's The backlog: triage from the page), which no longer fits now that triage always happens with an agent. mull has no way to do it: mull drop takes only inbox entries and items (`mull drop 7195` says not_found), and mull phase has only add, doc and list. 7195 has no items and nothing needs it, but it stays on the plan's board, in the tree and in mull context as a 0/0 phase, and since a phase is complete only once it has committed items, it never completes. A dropped phase should be recorded with its reason, like a dropped item, and leave the board; whether a phase with open items may be dropped (and what happens to them) is for Jeff to settle.
