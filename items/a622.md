Jeff's words, 2026-11-11: a nice global "shortcut" for me would be a way to quickly copy any ID with one click. maybe some kind of universal UI element beside every ID?

One small element, used everywhere mull serve shows an item, entry, phase or decision ID (tables, sheets, Needs you, the review packet, decisions, the tree): one click copies the bare ID to the clipboard and confirms in place. It's a copy action (DESIGN.md's Two Kinds of Action Rule), it must not get in the way of the ID's link or of selecting text, and it must stay out of the way so it doesn't clutter dense tables. IDs are how Jeff hands things to an agent.
