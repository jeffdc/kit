mull bug "…" and mull idea "…" capture an item (kind, free-text --category, --source, optional --plan/--phase). Items may have no plan (unplanned work). mull commit <id> [--plan --phase] moves captured to committed. Anyone captures; only the human commits (agents need --on-request).
