Jeff's words, 2026-11-11: another gap is that it is not clear if items that are Unplanned and done ever get fully closed out and archived.

How it works today: archiving exists only for plans. Closing a plan (plan close) puts it and everything in it in the tree's Archive, and search leaves it out unless asked (internal/mull/search.go, pages.go TreePage). Unplanned items have no plan to close, so they never reach the archive. Once done (accepted) or dropped, an unplanned item just ages out of the Unplanned list and the tree 14 days after it last changed, as long as no open entry is on it (doneShownFor and agedOut in internal/mull/pages.go). It stays reachable through Show all on Unplanned and its own sheet, and the default search still finds it forever, as if it were live. Nothing tells Jeff it's finished, and the 14-day fade looks the same as forgetting.

To settle with Jeff: whether an accepted or dropped unplanned item counts as archived (in the Archive, left out of default search, and marked archived on its sheet), and whether that happens right away or after the 14 days. Whether the same should apply to finished items in a plan that's still open. Whether the Archive in the tree gets an Unplanned group.
