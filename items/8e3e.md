mull done <phase-id> --summary --tour --design -, run on the target: runs the checks on the target tip, writes a report with the phase's combined diff and its items, and files a review entry on the phase. Accept completes the phase; do --reason creates a committed fix-up item in the phase with the reason, which reopens it. Refused when the phase has open items or no integration-review flag.

Update docs/design.md, the guides (internal/guide) and mull guide json wherever behaviour or output changes. The plan's doc (mull plan doc supervised-work) has the whole picture and what stays out of mull.
