Jeff (triage, 2026-11-23): we must be able to just work items from the backlog without reopening phases, and to lump several small captures into one piece of work rather than one session and merge each.
Today: mull start on a capture tagged to a complete phase commits it into that phase, which reopens it (the workaround was mull set <id> phase "" first, keeping it in the plan with no phase). And there's no way to work several captures as one item: the workaround was to fold them into one capture's notes and drop the rest as folded into it, which loses their own record.
Decide how both should work (e.g. start never reopens a complete phase, or a capture's phase tag is a pointer, not membership; a way to group captures into one item that keeps their IDs).
